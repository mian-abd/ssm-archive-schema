What is here

  • All 15 facilities, from each facility's first day on SSM (2018 for the oldest) to the archive pull in November 2026. A final pull after the last facility leaves SSM adds the remaining weeks.
  • Every view and column carries a description in Snowflake itself, so DESCRIBE VIEW or the object browser shows the same text as this page.

Keys

  • STORE_ID is the facility and is on almost every view. Join on it together with the other key.
  • LEASE_NUMBER is unique only within a facility: join leases on STORE_ID + LEASE_NUMBER.
  • TENANT_ID, UNIT_ID and USER_ID are unique across the company.
  • UNIT_TYPE_ID is shared across facilities: join unit types on STORE_ID + UNIT_TYPE_ID.
  • These are SSM's own IDs, not Cubby IDs. Cubby's legacy_id columns may carry them for imported records; check a few before relying on that.

Current state, history and monthly reports

  • Views such as UNITS, TENANTS and CURRENT_RENT_ROLL hold the state at the archive pull.
  • History lives in event views: LEASE_EVENTS, UNIT_STATUS_HISTORY, TENANT_CHANGE_HISTORY, RENT_CHANGES, LEDGER_ENTRIES, PAYMENTS.
  • The MONTHLY_* views are SSM's own month-end reports, one per facility per month. Use them to compare against SSM's numbers; use the event views to rebuild anything daily.

Money

  • LEDGER_ENTRIES is the tenant ledger: one row per charge, payment or credit on a lease. Positive amounts add to what the tenant owes.
  • PAYMENTS signs reversals and refunds as negative rows. A payment that covers several leases repeats; exclude IS_REPEATED_ROW rows when summing.

Known gap

  • SSM's inquiry-updates report fails on 987 store-days before February 2025, so lead status history in INQUIRY_UPDATES is missing for those days. The inquiries themselves are complete.
